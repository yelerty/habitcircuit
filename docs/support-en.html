Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

Q. How do I add a routine?

Click the ✏️ (edit) icon in the top right, or long press a routine for 0.5 seconds to enter edit mode. From the edit screen, you can add new routines.

Q. How do I change a routine's icon?

In the edit screen, click the pencil icon next to a routine to display the category icon selector. You can choose from 9 different category icons.

Q. How do I set up notifications?

In the Settings screen, you can set notification times for Morning, Afternoon, and Evening. When enabled, you'll receive routine reminders at your specified times.

Q. How do I share routines with others?

Go to Settings > "Web Sharing" to open the routine sharing webpage. Select your routines and share them to generate a QR code.

Q. How do I import someone else's routines?

There are two ways:
1) On the web sharing page, select a routine and tap "📤 Share" then "Add to HabitCircuit"
2) Go to Settings > "Import Routines" and select a JSON file

Q. Are routine execution times restricted?

Yes. Each routine can be executed from 1 hour before to 3 hours after the notification time. For example, if your morning notification is at 7:00 AM, you can execute morning routines between 6:00 AM and 10:00 AM.

Q. Can I save routines and load them later?

Yes! In Settings > "Manage Saved Routines", you can save and load up to 5 routine sets. Perfect for managing weekday and weekend routines separately.

Q. How do I delete all routines at once?

Go to Settings > "Manage Saved Routines" > "Delete All Current Routines" button to remove all routines.

Q. My streak was reset!

Streaks require completing all routines every day. Missing even one day will reset your current streak, but your longest record is preserved.

Q. Where is my data stored?

All data is securely stored locally on your iPhone/iPad. Nothing is sent to servers, though it may be included in iCloud backups.

Key Features Guide

🎯 Adding and Editing Routines

  1. Click the ✏️ icon in the top right
  2. Select a time slot (Morning/Afternoon/Evening)
  3. Choose a category icon
  4. Enter routine name and add
  5. Drag to reorder routines

🔥 Executing Routines

  1. Click "Start Routine" button on home screen
  2. Complete current routines one by one
  3. Mark complete with check button
  4. See celebration screen when all routines are done

💾 Backup and Restore Routines

  1. Settings > "Export Routines" to save as JSON file
  2. Settings > "Import Routines" to restore
  3. Sync routines across multiple devices

Troubleshooting

Notifications aren't working

1. Check notification permissions: iPhone Settings > Notifications > HabitCircuit
2. Verify notifications are enabled in app Settings for each time slot
3. Check if Do Not Disturb mode is enabled

My routines disappeared

1. Check if the correct day and time slot are selected
2. Verify they weren't accidentally deleted
3. If you have a backup file, restore using "Import Routines"

App is slow or freezing

1. Force quit the app and restart
2. Restart your iPhone/iPad
3. Update to the latest version
4. If issues persist, contact us using the information below
